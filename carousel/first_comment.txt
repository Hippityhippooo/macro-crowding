Code, data build and the pre-registration are here: [REPO URL]. The pre-registration commit is 5ae91c8, the development result is b3ce22d and the holdout is e9da60b. I committed the development result before the holdout ran, so you can check the order yourself.

The caveat I'd check first if this were someone else's post: prices are from Yahoo Finance, so most delisted firms are missing (94 of 6,274 covered). Their crowded-session share was 14.6% against 15.9% for survivors, so the gap is small, but it's there.

What's under the hood: events from EDGAR submissions with time of day from the acceptance stamp; crowding defined by trading session, not calendar day; two surprise measures (first-filed GAAP EPS vs last year, and the two-day market reaction); FF5 plus momentum abnormal returns; firm and year-quarter fixed effects with errors clustered by firm and reaction day; 1,000 random-calendar placebos; calendar-time long-short books net of estimated bid-ask spreads (Corwin-Schultz); 17 unit tests on synthetic EDGAR fixtures. I drafted the code with AI assistance and reviewed and ran it myself.
