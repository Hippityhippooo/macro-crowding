FOMC days carry 126 earnings releases on average. Ordinary days carry 72. Does the market read them any differently?

I timed every US earnings 8-K since 2005 (421,885) to the second from EDGAR and tested whether releases sharing a session with CPI, jobs or the Fed drift more, and whether firms park bad quarters there.

Pre-registered, holdout run once. What replicates is zero. The drift attention theory predicts is there, 3.6x larger in illiquid names; the calendar does not move it. What bad news shifts is the filing: late 8-Ks and Friday nights.
