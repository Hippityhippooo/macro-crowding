Fed days carry 126 earnings releases. Normal days, 72. Does the market read them differently?

I timed every US earnings 8-K since 2005 (421,885) to the second on EDGAR and asked: how crowded is the calendar, does crowding change the drift, and do firms park bad quarters there.

Thresholds written first; 2016–2025 held out, run once. My first sample found an effect on Fed days. The holdout killed it. My read: the market prices a Fed-day release like any other. Firms don't hide bad quarters behind the Fed; what they delay is the 8-K.

Do you trade a Fed-day release differently? I want to know.
