FOMC days carry 126 earnings releases. Normal days carry 72. Does the market read them any differently?

I timed every US earnings 8-K since 2005 (421,885) to the second from EDGAR and asked four things: how crowded is the calendar, does crowding change the drift, do firms park bad quarters there, can you trade it.

Thresholds written first; 2016–2025 held out, run once. My first sample found an effect on Fed days. The holdout killed it. Firms don't hide bad quarters behind the Fed; they delay the 8-K. Nothing covers its costs.

Do you treat a Fed-day release differently? I'd actually like to know.
