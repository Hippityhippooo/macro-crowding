FOMC days carry 126 earnings releases on average. Ordinary days carry 72. Does the market read them any differently?

I built every US earnings 8-K since 2005 from EDGAR (421,885), timed each to the second, and tested whether releases that share a session with CPI, the jobs report or the Fed drift more afterwards, and whether firms park bad quarters there.

Pre-registered, holdout run once. The development sample said less drift on Fed days; the holdout said no. What replicates is zero, and bad quarters are spread evenly.

If you trade earnings: do you treat a Fed-day release any differently?
