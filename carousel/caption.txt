FOMC days carry 126 earnings releases. Normal days carry 72. I wanted to know if that changes anything.

So I pulled every US earnings 8-K since 2005, 421,885 of them, timed each to the second from EDGAR, and tested two things: do releases sharing a session with CPI, jobs or the Fed drift more, and do firms park bad quarters on those days.

Thresholds written down first; 2016–2025 held out and run once. My first sample found an effect on Fed days. The holdout killed it. Firms don't hide bad quarters behind the Fed either; what they delay is the 8-K.

Do you treat a Fed-day release differently? I'd actually like to know.
